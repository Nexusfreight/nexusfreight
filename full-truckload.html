How far ahead should I book a truckload?+
Same-day and next-day trucks are often possible on Midwest lanes, but 24–48 hours' notice usually gets a better rate and a better carrier. For weekly freight, ask about a contract rate.
Do you offer contract rates?+
Yes, for lanes you ship regularly. We price them on your volume and pickup pattern, then hold the same contact on every load.
How much fits in a 53′ dry van?+
Typically 26 standard pallets single-stacked and up to about 45,000 lb of freight, depending on the carrier's tractor and the axle weights.

What do you need for a quote?+

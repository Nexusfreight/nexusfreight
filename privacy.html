Who we are

This policy explains how Nexus Freight Management Inc (“Nexus”, “we”, “us”), 768 N Industrial Dr, Elmhurst, IL 60126, collects and uses personal information through nexusfreight.com (the “Site”). Questions: sales@nexusfreight.com or (847) 306-3637.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote or contact us: your name, company, work email, phone number, and shipment details such as pickup and delivery ZIP codes, dates, equipment, commodity and weight.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, the Site and our hosting provider record technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and the referring page. If we use analytics, it also uses cookies or similar technology [confirm: Google Analytics 4 or none].

We do not ask for, and you should not send through the Site, payment card numbers, bank details or government ID numbers.

How we use it

  • To answer your request, prepare a rate and book and manage your shipments.
  • To contact you about loads, quotes and our services. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To run, secure and improve the Site, and to understand which pages are useful.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations, and to protect our rights.

How we share it

We share personal information only as needed to provide the service:

  • Motor carriers booked on your load receive the pickup and delivery details and contacts they need to haul it.
  • Service providers that host the Site, deliver email, provide analytics or support our business systems, under contracts that limit their use of the data.
  • Authorities when required by law, or to protect safety and prevent fraud.
  • A buyer or successor if the business is sold or reorganized.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Cookies

The Site uses only the cookies and local storage it needs to work (for example, remembering that you paused the background video) [plus analytics cookies, if analytics is added]. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the Site will still work.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and shipment records as long as needed for the business relationship and for legal, tax and claims purposes [e.g. up to 7 years for shipment records; confirm], then delete or anonymize them.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing sales@nexusfreight.com. Depending on your state of residence, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws; we will respond as those laws require. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

Children

The Site is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Other websites

The Site links to other sites, such as the FMCSA SAFER database and Highway for carrier onboarding. Their privacy practices are their own.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will be noted on this page.

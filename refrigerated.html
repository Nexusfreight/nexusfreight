How do you make sure the temperature holds?+
The set point and run mode are written on the rate confirmation, the carrier pre-cools before loading, and temperatures are checked in transit. A full download is available on request [confirm].
What happens if a load is rejected for temperature?+
[Confirm with Nexus: the claims process and who handles it.]
Can you ship temperature-controlled LTL?+
Yes. Smaller reefer shipments can move as temp-controlled LTL or a partial, so you don't pay for a whole trailer.
Do you offer protect-from-freeze in winter?+
Yes. In winter a reefer can run in heat mode to keep freight such as beverages or liquids from freezing.
Are the trailers food-grade?+
We book clean, odor-free, food-grade trailers, and a washout can be requested before loading.
